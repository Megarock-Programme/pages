Megabots help

Megabots is your company's AI assistant. What it can reach (models, agents, company data) is set by your admin. If something you need is missing, ask them: their names are on the "You don't have access" screens and behind every "Ask your admin" button.

Getting started

  • New chat starts a conversation. Pick an agent and a model under the message box before your first message: the agent stays with the conversation.
  • Agents are assistants set up for a job, each with its own tools and data. The default one, Megabots, is for everyday questions.
  • Projects group conversations and share files and notes across them.
  • All conversations lists every chat, including the ones you had in a chat app.

Chat apps

Your admin decides which chat apps Megabots answers in. The ones you can use are listed in Settings › Account.

Telegram and WhatsApp

  1. Open the Megabots bot (Telegram) or message the Megabots number (WhatsApp).
  2. Send /login your@email, with the email you sign in to Megabots with.
  3. Send the 6-digit code we email you. You stay signed in on that app.

Slack

There is no sign-in step. Open Megabots under Apps in your company Slack and send it a message, or @mention it in a channel. Your Slack email must be the one you use for Megabots.

Discord

  1. In a server that has Megabots, or in a DM with it, run /login with your email.
  2. Run /otp with the code we email you.

Your company data

Data Hub holds the databases and warehouses the assistant can query, and Knowledge base the documents it can search. You see the ones your role can read in Settings. Ask a question in chat and the assistant answers from live data. To get access to another source, ask your admin.

Admins: see Connecting a source to Data Hub.

Routines

A routine sends a prompt for you on a schedule, for example a sales summary every Monday at 8:00, and keeps the answers in one conversation. Create one from Routines in the sidebar, or from the menu on any reply. How many you may have is set by your role.

Voice

Press the microphone to record a message: the text lands in the message box for you to check before sending. Press Listen under a reply to hear it. Choose the voice in Settings › Personalization.

Connectors

Connect your own apps (Gmail, Google Drive, Notion and others) or an MCP server under Connectors in the sidebar. A connection is yours alone; nobody else's chats use it.

Usage and limits

Settings › Usage shows what you have used and what it cost. Your role may have a daily or monthly limit; when you reach it, chat pauses until the next period.

Settings

  • Account: your profile, password, and how to reach Megabots in chat apps.
  • Personalization: theme, voice and memory.
  • Capabilities: what your organization has switched on, such as web search or the code interpreter.
  • Agents, Data Hub, Knowledge base, Message channels: what your organization has set up. Admins change them here.

A control that is greyed with "Turned off by your administrator" exists, but your organization has switched it off.
